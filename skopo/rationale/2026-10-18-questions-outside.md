The pilot kept its notes and its questions outside the skopository on purpose, because "questions
and evaluation are not intent". The specification adds a second reason: the disagreements
a generator finds can reveal weaknesses in the system it reads, and the skopository may be shared
more widely than the generator's report, so the report stays confidential to the people the
generator works for.
