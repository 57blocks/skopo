Draft 0.5 set the axiom "write only what cannot be recomputed cheaply": provenance became optional
and generated views stopped being stored. The reason given is that models will soon recompute what
code does, while what people decided cannot be recomputed. The check that demanded a change record
for every rule was dropped with it, which is why its name stays unused.
