Only a bold keyword carries force, following RFC 8174, where only capitals do; each language's list
is modelled on the standard its engineers already know (RFC 2119 for English, GB/T 1.1-2020 for
Chinese). Recommendations are left out of the keywords because "an agent cannot build from one".
Because the checks look for bold text rather than for words, they work in languages without word
boundaries.
