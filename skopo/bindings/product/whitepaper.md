# The whitepaper: product binding

*When agents write the code, what do we write?* as its readers meet it. Its consumers are people deciding whether Skopo is worth
a closer look: engineering leads, architects and product managers.

- The whitepaper is written in Chinese and in English; the Chinese text is written first. ^F-4h23dm
- A reader **may** switch the page between Chinese and English, **may** choose a dark, light or
  system theme through the page's address, and **may** switch each skopository sample between its
  rendered form and its raw source. ^F-nvjbz0
- Until a reader chooses, the page opens in the first of the reader's browser languages that is
  Chinese or English, and in English when there is none. ^F-yav9n4
- The page **must not** take its Markdown from an address in its parameters unless it is served from
  the reader's own machine or opened from disk. ^F-d8pmdm
- The whitepaper's text is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  licence, and the page that renders it under the Apache License 2.0. ^F-wqdfjy
- A self-contained copy of the page for each language opens from disk without a server and prints to
  PDF, each section on a new page. ^F-m5m5bj
