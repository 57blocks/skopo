# Lending: charter

## Words

- A **member** is a person the library has registered to borrow books. ^T-0b7199
- A **loan** is one book lent to one member, from the day it is lent until it comes back. ^T-aq165k
- A loan is **overdue** when its due date has passed and the book is not back. ^T-kx1kt6

## Principles

- A book **must not** be lent while it is on an open loan. ^R-cxsjfa
- A member with an overdue loan **must not** borrow another book. ^R-5tps87
- The library **must not** charge a member for returning a book late. ^R-3bpfef

> *Example.* A member returns a book a week late. Nothing is charged, and the member can borrow again the same day. ^E-g1j5t8 (R-3bpfef, R-5tps87)
